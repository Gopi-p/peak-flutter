Privacy Policy

Peak · Last updated 16 June 2026

Peak is a personal workout tracker built to keep your data entirely on your own device. This policy explains, plainly, what we do and don't do with your information. The short version: we don't collect any of it.

Data we collect

None. Peak has no user accounts, no login, and no servers. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, usage data, analytics, or device identifiers.

Where your data lives

  • Everything you log — workouts, sets, body weight, goals, and personal records — is stored in a single database file on your device.
  • That data never leaves your phone unless you choose to export it.
  • If you have iCloud Backup enabled, your device backup (which may include Peak's local database) is managed entirely by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. Peak itself does not upload anything.

Export and import

Peak lets you export your data to a JSON file and import it back. These actions are initiated by you and happen locally through the iOS file system. The exported file goes wherever you choose to save or share it.

Photos

Peak does not use your photo library. A photo-access permission string may appear in the app because a file-selection library Peak uses includes that capability, but Peak only ever uses it to pick JSON files and never reads your photos.

Third parties

Peak contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking SDKs. Tutorial links may open YouTube or your browser; once you leave Peak, the privacy policy of that service applies.

Children's privacy

Peak does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

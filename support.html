Common questions

Where is my data stored?
Entirely on your device. Peak has no accounts and no servers. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I back up or move my data?
Use Settings → Export to save a JSON file, and Import to restore it on any device. iCloud device backups also include your data automatically.

I reinstalled and my data is gone.
Each install starts fresh. Restore from a JSON export or an iCloud device backup if you have one.
